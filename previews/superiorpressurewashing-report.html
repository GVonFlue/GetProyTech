The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

The homepage weighs 10.4 MB

That is a very heavy page, and one image alone is 1.8 MB. On a good desktop connection it loaded quickly for us. On a phone with a weak signal, which is how most people look up a pressure washer, a page this size is slow and eats data.

The fix: Photos sized for the screen they are shown on, so the page is a fraction of the weight.
2

Template leftovers are still in the code

Every page we checked still carries the original template's placeholder phone number (+1 123 456 7890), the address info@example.com, and sample testimonials signed Jane D, John S and Emily R. They were hidden in the desktop view we tested, but Google and AI tools read the code, not just what is on screen.

The fix: A clean build with only your real phone, your real email and your real reviews.
3

Your best proof is not what people see first

The first thing on the page is a stock photo of a house. Your own before and after photos are far more convincing. The reviews section also says 76 Google reviews when you now have 82, and the footer says 2024.

The fix: Lead with your real before and after jobs, and pull the review count live.
