The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

The homepage has no phone number

We found no phone number, no address and no hours anywhere on the homepage, and no way to tap to call. Someone with a car problem has to find the Contact Us page before they can reach you.

The fix: Your number, address and warranty in the first screen, and a call button that follows the page.
2

The map on your contact page is broken

Where the map should be, the Contact Us page shows "Oops! Something went wrong. This page didn't load Google Maps correctly." The footer on every page reads Copyright 2019.

The fix: Your address written out, with a working directions link.
3

Your best selling points are two clicks deep

The 2 year, 24,000 mile warranty, the Hunter alignment machines and the factory software updates are on the Services page. Your 206 Google reviews are not on the site at all.

The fix: Warranty, equipment and reviews on the homepage.
