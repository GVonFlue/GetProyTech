The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

The headline is cut off

The first line on your homepage reads "Professional Painting Company Serving the Wichita, KS Greater Metro Are." The last letter is missing. It is the first thing every visitor reads.

The fix: A headline that leads with 50 years, spelled right.
2

Stock photos up front, your real work hidden

Every photo on the homepage is a stock image: paint rollers, a compass, a model in white coveralls. Your own jobs are on a separate page, and there are only four of them.

The fix: Your yellow two story as the first thing people see, and your work on the homepage.
3

28 five star reviews and none on the site

Customers on Google say they hired you for a second and third house. The site mentions referrals are available but shows no reviews and no rating.

The fix: Your 5.0 rating up top and six real reviews, word for word.
