Common questions.

Do you do residential driveways or only commercial lots?

Both. Petra Asphalt works on commercial parking lots and residential driveways.

What areas do you serve?

Wichita, Derby, Haysville, Hutchinson, McPherson and communities across south‑central Kansas.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Send the address and what needs attention, or call or text (316) 285‑9467, and Braydon will follow up to look at the pavement.

Can the work be done without shutting down my business?

Projects are scheduled around weather, access and traffic. Tell us your busy hours when you request the estimate.

Should cracks be filled before sealcoating?

Usually, yes. Filling suitable cracks first helps keep water out of the base, and the sealcoat then protects the whole surface. Your estimate will spell out what your pavement needs.

Can you redo ADA markings and fire lanes?

Yes. Striping covers parking lines, ADA markings, access aisles, arrows, curbs and fire lanes.
