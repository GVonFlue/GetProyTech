The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

Your website did not open

We opened arpaintco.com in a current version of Chrome on November 17 and got a browser error page, on both the homepage and the contact page. An earlier check by our team found the cause: the secure version of your address fails its security certificate, and only the older unsecured address loads. Many browsers now refuse unsecured addresses.

The fix: A site on a secure address that opens in every browser.
2

It was not built for phones

That earlier check also found the site has no mobile layout, so it scrolls sideways, four images fail to load, and the phone number is plain text that cannot be tapped to call.

The fix: A page built for phones first, with a call button on every screen.
3

Your best work is only on Google

Your Google profile has sharp photos of finished houses and 41 five star reviews. None of that reaches someone who clicks through to your site.

The fix: Your own job photos and six real reviews on the homepage.
