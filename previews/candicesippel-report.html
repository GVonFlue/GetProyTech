The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

It lives on a Canva address, not Henning Real Estate's

henningrealestate.my.canva.site belongs to Canva. As a brand-new brokerage, a domain of your own is what makes Henning Real Estate look established and lets you build search history that is yours.

The fix: Your own domain, keeping the colors and photo you already chose.
2

No home search and no request form

Visitors can read about you, but they cannot search homes or send you a note. Your email and text number are at the very bottom.

The fix: A three-way "which move are you making?" chooser, a request form and text-first contact at the top.
3

Small details undercut a new brokerage

The page title is just "Candice". The reviews section heading shows no reviews in the page text, "Area's of Service" has an apostrophe it doesn't need, and the degree line reads "with and emphasis".

The fix: Clean copy, a real page title, and your 34 Google reviews actually shown.
