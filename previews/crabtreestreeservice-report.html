The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

The free quote button does not do anything

The big purple button says to call for a free quote, but it is not connected to anything. On a phone you cannot tap it to call, and there are no tap to call links anywhere on the page.

The fix: A real tap to call button, tap to text, and a quote form on every screen.
2

No way to send a request in writing

There is no contact form and no email on the site. Anyone who finds you after hours and does not want to call has no next step.

The fix: A free quote form that goes straight to Steve, and an assistant that takes the details at night.
3

403 reviews and Steve himself are missing

The page has about 150 words and a stock photo of a chainsaw. Nothing about removals near houses, emergency work, or the way Steve answers every review.

The fix: Real job photos, real reviews, and Steve in his own words.
