The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

No phone number, form or booking anywhere on the page

A visitor who wants service has no way to reach you from the homepage. There is no phone number, no contact form and no request button. They go back to Google and call the next company.

The fix: Your number in the header and a sticky call, text and request bar on phones.
2

It doesn't say what you do

The page is your name, one large stock photo of someone else's plumbing, a search box and a list of blog posts. Nothing says you do plumbing, electric and heating and air, or that you serve Park City and Wichita.

The fix: A clear headline, your three trades and the calls you take every week, all above the fold.
3

Your best proof is missing

59 reviews at 4.7, real photos of your crew, your shop and your vans, and owner Patrick by name: none of it is on the site. A stock photo tells a customer nothing about who is coming to their house.

The fix: Your real team and shop photos, and reviews in your customers' own words.
