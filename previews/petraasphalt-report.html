The 3 things costing you jobs right now

1

Your site tells most AI crawlers to stay out.

More property managers and homeowners now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or their phone's assistant "who does parking lot sealcoating in Wichita." Your site's robots.txt (the file that tells crawlers what they can read) blocks 32 of them by name, including ClaudeBot, GPTBot, Google‑Extended (Gemini), Applebot‑Extended (Apple) and Meta's crawler. It looks like Cloudflare's built‑in AI blocking setting rather than a choice made for Petra, and it's a quick fix.

In your preview: built to be read by AI search, with a plain‑language FAQ, your service area and your reviews in a form these tools can quote. On launch, robots.txt welcomes them.
2

On a phone, there's no menu.

On a phone screen the header hides Home, Services, Our Work, About and your phone number, and there's no menu button to bring them back. A visitor sees your logo and "Free Estimate," and nothing else at the top. To see your before and afters or call you, they have to scroll and hunt.

In your preview: a real phone menu, plus a bar pinned to the bottom of the screen with Call, Text and Free Estimate.
3

Your 5.0 Google rating is nowhere on the site.

Google shows Petra Asphalt at 5.0 stars from 8 reviews, with lines like "The before and after was astonishing" and "They also use Superior products." Your site doesn't show a single review or the rating. For someone choosing between three contractors, that's the deciding factor, and right now they have to leave your site to find it.

In your preview: "5.0 on Google" in the first screen, and 7 real reviews with first names, in their own words.

Everything we checked

Up and secure

Good
  • HTTPS, served through Cloudflare, no warnings.
  • Every page in the menu loads, and a mistyped address returns a proper "not found" page.

Speed

Good

Mobile experience

Broken
  • No menu on phones: the navigation links and the phone number are hidden in the header, and there's no menu button. (see #2)
  • No call or text button that stays on screen as people scroll.

Why it matters: most people looking up a contractor are on their phone, often while standing in the lot they want fixed.

First impression

Good
  • Strong headline, your own drone photo, and two clear buttons. It's obvious what you do within seconds.
  • Small gap: the first screen doesn't say where you work. "South‑central Kansas" only shows up in the footer.

Turning visits into calls

Needs work
  • Good: a clear estimate form, click‑to‑call, and a simple 3‑step "what to expect."
  • The estimate page says "Prefer to call or text?" but there's only a call link, no tap‑to‑text.
  • No way to send photos of the lot with the request, which is often what you need to quote.
  • Nothing answers after hours. A property manager filling out work orders at 9pm gets a form and a wait.
  • We didn't submit the form, so we couldn't confirm where requests go. Worth a test submission.

Trust and reviews

Missing
  • No reviews or rating anywhere on the site, despite 5.0 from 8 on Google. (see #3)
  • The About page tells your story but has no photo of you or the crew. People hire the person.
  • Nothing about insurance, years in business or the products you use (a reviewer mentioned your Superior products).

Google search

Needs work
  • Good foundations: a unique title and description on every page, LocalBusiness schema with your name, phone and 5 service cities, and a sitemap.
  • No pages for the cities you serve. Your schema lists Wichita, Derby, Haysville, Hutchinson and McPherson, but there's no page for any of them (petraasphalt.com/wichita is a 404).
  • No canonical tags, which help Google pick the right version of each page.
  • In web searches we ran on November 5 for "best sealcoating company Wichita KS," "parking lot striping and crack filling Wichita Kansas," and even "Petra Asphalt" Wichita, petraasphalt.com didn't come up. The site is new (September 2026), so this is normal, but it won't fix itself without city pages and reviews.
  • Name confusion: on Google Maps, "PETRA" (an investment company with 34 reviews) and "PETRA Services" show up right beside you.

AI search (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Apple)

Blocked
  • robots.txt blocks ClaudeBot, GPTBot, Google‑Extended, Applebot‑Extended, Amazonbot, Meta's crawler and 26 others. (see #1)
  • No llms.txt (the short summary AI tools look for).
  • No FAQ. AI tools quote plain answers to plain questions, like "how often should a parking lot be sealcoated."

Link previews

Missing
  • No Open Graph tags, so when you text or post petraasphalt.com there's no photo card, just a bare link. That's every link you send a property manager.

Accessibility

Good
  • Every image we checked has a description, form fields are labeled, and text contrast is strong. Spot‑checked, not a full audit.

Broken or outdated

None found
  • No dead links in the menu, no placeholder text, and a current copyright year.

Tracking

Needs work
  • Cloudflare's basic visitor counts are on. No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, and no Search Console tag found (it may be verified another way). You can't see which searches bring people in or which pages lead to calls.
