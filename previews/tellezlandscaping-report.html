The 3 problems costing you jobs right now

1

Your website shows "Website Expired"

The address in your Instagram bio, tellezlandscaping.net, loads a Squarespace page that says the account has expired. Homeowners who tap it from Instagram see that instead of your work, and most won't try again.

Fix: a fast, mobile first site on a domain you own, live in days, with your photos and reviews on it.
2

Your Google listing has no website

Your Google Business Profile is strong: 4.9 stars, 56 reviews, real photos. But the website field is empty, so people comparing landscapers can't click through to see services, a project gallery or a way to request an estimate. Google also uses your website to decide when to show you, so an empty field holds your ranking back.

Fix: link the new site from your Google listing, with your name, phone and service area matching exactly.
3

Leads that come in after hours have nowhere to go

Landscaping customers shop at night and on weekends, often from their phone while looking at their bare yard. Right now the only option is calling during the day. If you're on a job and miss the call, that homeowner calls the next company on Google.

Fix: a yard plan form and a 24/7 assistant that collects the project details and texts them straight to you.
