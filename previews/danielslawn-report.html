The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

The phone link on your contact page dials the wrong number

On the contact page the number reads 316-253-1782. Tap it on a phone and it dials 316-256-1482. Two digits are different. The Call Us button at the top of the page dials the right number, so this is easy to miss, but anyone who taps the number in the page body reaches someone else.

The fix: One phone number, checked, on every button and link.
2

The Email Us button copies a second address

The Email Us links in the header send to your Gmail address and also to a Yahoo address that is not shown anywhere on the site. If that address is old or belongs to someone who no longer works with you, customer emails are going to an inbox you may not see. It is worth checking.

The fix: Email links that go only where you want them to.
3

Visitors cannot zoom on a phone

The site is set to block pinch to zoom. Anyone who wants a closer look at a project photo, or who needs larger text, cannot get it. The footer also still reads 2013 to 2024.

The fix: Zoom allowed, photos that open large, and a current footer.
