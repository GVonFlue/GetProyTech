What we build

Every part of the yard, one crew.

Most of our work is full front and back yard packages for new homes, plus upgrades for yards that need a second life.

Xeriscape & rock

Low water designs with river rock, boulders and plants that handle Colorado sun, wind and hail without a sky high water bill.

Sod & lawns

Graded, prepped and laid right so the lawn drains away from the house and stays green.

Flagstone, pavers & patios

Flagstone paths, brick and paver walkways, and brushed concrete patios finished on the date we promise.

Trees, shrubs & beds

We help you pick trees and plants that will actually thrive here, then plant, mulch and edge them.

New construction yards

Bare dirt to finished yard, front and back. Design, HOA paperwork and install handled in one go.

And the hard stuff

The jobs other crews skip.

  • Retaining walls and garden steps
  • Grading and resloping for drainage
  • Sprinkler fixes, including backflow valves
  • Raised beds on rocky hillsides
  • Stump removal and reseeding
