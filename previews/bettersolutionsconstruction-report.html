The 3 problems costing you jobs right now

1

Your 158 five‑star reviews are invisible.

Google shows 4.9 stars from 158 reviews. Your homepage shows the same 6 reviews on repeat. For a roofer, reviews are the deciding factor, and the biggest reason to pick you is buried.

In your preview: 4.9 from 158 Google reviews right in the first screen, plus real recent reviews by name.
2

On a phone, a financing pop‑up covers the page.

Google's phone test shows a financing pop‑up over the whole screen when the page opens, and the main content takes 4.1 seconds to appear. Someone with water coming through the ceiling isn't going to wait or hunt for the close button. There's also no call button that stays on screen as they scroll.

In your preview: no pop‑up, financing moved to a calm spot by the inspection form, a lighter photo hero instead of video, and the phone number stays at the top of the screen.
3

Google and AI don't know who you are yet.

We searched "best roofing company Wichita KS" and a storm‑damage, insurance and Spanish‑speaking search. Better Solutions didn't come up in either. The site has no business schema telling Google you're a roofer, no pages for the 8 cities you serve, and no tracking, so there's no way to see what's working.

In your preview: roofing contractor schema with your rating, hours, languages and service area built in, plus clear insurance and Spanish content AI tools can quote.

Everything we found

Up and secure

Good
  • HTTPS works, no warnings, and your Google profile links to the right site.
  • Your domain is a .org, which people associate with nonprofits, and bettersolutionsconstruction.com belongs to someone else. bettersolutionsroofing.com is available.

Speed on phones

Needs work
  • Main content takes 4.1 seconds on a phone, and the page downloads 4.4 MB.
  • The top of the page is a stock video clip. It's the heaviest thing on the page and it isn't your crew.
  • Google estimates about 327 KB of unused code and 282 KB of savings from better image formats.

Why it matters: storm damage calls come from phones, often on cell signal, often in a hurry.

Mobile experience

Needs work
  • A financing pop‑up covers the screen when the page opens.
  • The phone number is clickable, but there's no call button that stays visible while scrolling.

First impression

Needs work
  • The headline is "Better Solutions for a Better Tomorrow." It doesn't say roofing, Wichita, or why to call you.
  • Your 4.9 rating, your crew and your bilingual team don't appear in the first screen.

Turning visits into calls

Needs work
  • The quote form works, and phone links are clickable. That's good.
  • You offer 24/7 emergency service, but there's nothing on the site that answers or captures someone at night.
  • Insurance claims are one of your biggest strengths, but there's no clear "how it works" for homeowners who have never filed one.

Trust and reviews

Missing
  • 158 Google reviews at 4.9 stars aren't shown. The page repeats the same 6 reviews several times.
  • "Licensed and insured" appears, but with no details.
  • The crew photo only appears on the About page.

Google search

Needs work
  • No business schema (the code that tells Google you're a roofing contractor, your hours, rating and area).
  • The Services menu items aren't real links Google can follow, and 4 links say only "Learn More."
  • You list 8 cities but have no page for any of them, so you're unlikely to show up for "roofer in Hutchinson" or "roofer in Salina."
  • 7 of 58 images have no description, and the About page description has a typo ("Learns about").

AI search (ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity)

Needs work
  • Good news: your site already allows AI crawlers and has an llms.txt file.
  • But there are no plain answers to the questions people ask AI ("how does a hail claim work", "roofer who speaks Spanish in Wichita") and no FAQ.
  • Google's AI agent check scored 2 out of 3.

Link previews

Good
  • Your link shows an image and description when texted or shared. The title doesn't include your business name, which is an easy fix.

Accessibility

Good
  • Scored 96. The service dropdown on the form is missing a label for screen readers.

Broken or outdated

Minor
  • The browser logs errors while the page loads. Nothing visible to visitors.
  • Reviews are duplicated on the homepage, which looks like a glitch to anyone who scrolls.

Tracking

Missing
  • No Google Analytics and no Meta Pixel found. You can't see how many people visit, where they come from, or which pages lead to calls.
