The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

A Contact Us button leads to a missing page

A Contact Us button on your homepage points to trustroofingllc.com/get-a-quote-nueva. We opened it on November 17 and got "We couldn't find the page you were looking for." Every homeowner who presses it is lost.

The fix: One estimate form, on the homepage, that every button leads to.
2

Leftover template pieces

The Instagram and Facebook icons in the footer open Squarespace's own accounts, not yours. The footer still says 2022. The Financial Assistance page has no financing information on it.

The fix: Every link checked, the footer current, and no empty pages in the menu.
3

No way to know what the site is bringing in

We found no Google Analytics or Meta Pixel on the site. There is also no estimate form on the homepage, so there is nothing to count.

The fix: An estimate form with tracking, so you can see how many requests the site produces.
