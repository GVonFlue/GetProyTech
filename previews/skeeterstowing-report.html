The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

Nobody can tap to call

For a towing company the phone number is the whole website. We found no tap to call link on the homepage, so a stranded driver has to memorize the number and dial it.

The fix: A call button that follows the screen, plus one tap choices for a tow, a flat, fuel or a lockout.
2

A Wix ad sits on top of your business

The first thing on every page is a banner that says "This website was built on Wix. Create yours today." The address is a Wix subdomain, not your own name, and the footer still reads "2023 by Name of Site."

The fix: Your own domain, no ads, and a footer with your name on it.
3

Placeholder pieces were never filled in

The Facebook, Twitter and Instagram icons open Wix's accounts. An earlier check by our team found a "Pictures Coming Soon" graphic on the About page and an empty Gallery page.

The fix: Your real truck photos in the gallery and no dead ends.
