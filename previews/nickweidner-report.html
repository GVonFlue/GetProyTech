The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

Your domain forwards to your brokerage's site

nickwre.com sends visitors to nickw.reecenichols.com. The domain is yours, but the page is the brokerage's template, and the search history builds up on their address.

The fix: Your own site at nickwre.com, with your listings search linked in.
2

The first screen is a stock photo, not you

The top of the page is a stock kitchen, "There's no place like home" and three icon buttons. Your face, your name and your reviews are what make someone call you.

The fix: Your headshot, your line "Guiding you home" and your rating, right at the top.
3

Your reviews tell a story the site does not

Clients describe you at every step: patient with first-time buyers, showing the same house twice in a day, beating multiple offers, selling in six days. That is your best sales page.

The fix: A buyer and seller roadmap with a real review at each step.
