The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

Customers get a "Privacy error" warning

Opening https://dm-mechanical.com in Chrome on November 12 showed a full-page privacy error instead of your site. The http version also failed to load. Most people will not click past a warning like that.

The fix: A secure site with a valid certificate, on a domain your Google listing points to.
2

The look-alike domain is for sale

dmmechanical.com, the same name without the hyphen, opens a "for sale" page from a domain marketplace. Anyone who types your name without the hyphen lands there instead of on you.

The fix: Decide which domain is home, and consider securing the other so it forwards to you.
3

424 reviews and nowhere to show them

Your reviews mention Matt, Duane, Jesse and Mindy by name, 45 minute response on a holiday weekend, and honest options. None of that reaches a customer who clicks Website.

The fix: Your team, your reviews and your 24-hour line on a page that loads.
