The 3 problems costing you business right now

1

Your website lives on your brokerage's domain

The website on your Google profile is amypreister.kw.com, a page hosted on Keller Williams' domain. Every review, link and bit of search history you build there belongs to that address, not to Preister and Partners.

The fix: A Preister and Partners site on a domain you own, so it moves with you no matter what.
2

The Preister and Partners brand is not the star

You have a strong p&p monogram, a big team and real photos. A brokerage template treats every agent the same, so a visitor does not get that this is a team with 20 years behind it.

The fix: A site built around your monogram, Amy and the partners.
3

165 reviews and your Q&A posts have no home

You post real estate Q&A on Google and you have 165 five-star-average reviews. On a site you own, that content becomes pages that rank and answers AI tools can quote.

The fix: Reviews on the homepage, and a place for your Q&A posts to become search pages.
