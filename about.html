About

Two people who got tired of watching good businesses lose to bad systems.

ProyTech is Garrett Von Flue and Logan Sell. Two founders, no account managers, no call center. When you hire us you're working with the people who build the thing.

Garrett came up through the Army, then diesel mechanics and welding, then real estate, and spent years watching businesses with great people lose deals to nothing more than a missed call and a follow up nobody sent. Logan handles delivery and build. Between the two of them the work goes from a conversation to something live in about two weeks.

We're based in Wichita and we build for businesses across the country. Everything we do works the same over a video call as it does across a table.

How we work

A few things we don't negotiate on.

Give first

The teardown is free and you keep it whether or not you hire us. We'd rather be useful to a hundred people and work with ten than pitch all hundred.

No hard sell, ever

If we're not the right fit we'll say so on the first call and point you somewhere better. Talking somebody into this helps nobody.

Build only what's sold

We don't ship features looking for a use. Everything we build is because a real business needed it to run.

Tell you the truth about timelines

Most builds are live in 7 to 14 days. If yours is bigger, we scope it and tell you up front rather than discovering it halfway through.

In the community

Suite Nights started because networking events are usually miserable and business owners deserve better than a name tag and a cash bar. We rent a suite, fill it with people worth knowing, and get out of the way.

We've done Military Suite Night at Equity Bank Park and Opening Night with the Wichita Thunder, and we sponsor, connect and hand out more than we ask for. It isn't a lead strategy. It's just the version of this business we'd want to be part of.
