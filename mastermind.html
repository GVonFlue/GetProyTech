Questions

Good to know.

Who is this for?

Wichita business owners who want to step away and work on the business instead of in it. Tables mix newer and established owners on purpose, so there's something to learn in both directions.

Why only 10 seats?

Mastermind tables only work when everyone gets real airtime. Ten owners keeps it small enough that every person's business gets worked on.

Is it all work?

No. Sessions run in the mornings and early afternoons on the two sea days. Evenings, the island day and the Tampa day are yours.

Can I bring my spouse or business partner?

Yes. They can share your balcony stateroom at double occupancy pricing. Reach out to Scott for the current price.

Do I need a passport?

For a round trip cruise from Tampa to the Bahamas, US citizens can often travel with a birth certificate and photo ID, but a passport is strongly recommended. Scott will confirm requirements when you book.

Who do I talk to about booking?

Scott Temaat at Squared Away Travel handles all travel and payments. Call (316) 375-2100 or email scott@squaredawaytravel.com.
