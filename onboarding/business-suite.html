First, the basics.

Four quick ones. The whole thing takes about ten minutes.

Every website you have.

The suite has to talk to all of them, not just the main one.

Worth slowing down here. Every site you own is a place a lead can come in from. If we don't know a site exists, its enquiries never reach the system — they keep landing in an inbox nobody checks. Separate reviews sites, old sites, ad landing pages and booking pages all count.

How you keep track right now.

Be honest — “a notebook and my memory” is a completely normal answer, and it's easier to work with than a half-abandoned system.

The stages a job moves through.

These become the columns on your board — where every lead sits at a glance.

We've filled in the usual ones. Don't start from a blank page. Change the wording to match what you'd actually say out loud, delete any you don't use, add the ones we missed. If a customer would recognise the word, it's the right word.

Who's on the team.

And, more to the point, what each of them should be able to see.

The access column is the one to think about. Most owners don't want the crew seeing what a job sold for, or one salesperson seeing another's leads. Set it here and we build it in from day one — it's fiddly to unpick later.

How it should sound.

The system sends quotes, reminders and follow-ups in your name. Last step.
