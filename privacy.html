ProyTech Business Solutions LLC ("ProyTech," "we," "us") is a Kansas company based in Wichita that builds websites, a custom CRM called the Business Suite, and marketing automations for businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. This policy covers getproytech.com and its pages, our booking and contact forms, our proposals and electronic signing, client onboarding, the ProyTech CRM we use to run our own business, and our text and email communications. It also explains our role when we host a Business Suite or website for a client (Section 7). This policy does not cover websites or Business Suites we build for clients, which are governed by each client's own privacy policy, or third-party sites we link to.

2How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  1. respond to inquiries, schedule meetings and provide consultations;
  2. prepare, send and record acceptance of proposals;
  3. onboard clients and build and deliver the Services;
  4. process payments, send invoices and manage accounts;
  5. send service messages, project updates and support responses;
  6. send marketing emails and texts where permitted, with opt-out options;
  7. draft content, summaries and follow-ups using AI tools (Section 4);
  8. operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve our website, CRM and Services;
  9. run our referral (Partner) program and events; and
  10. comply with law, enforce our Terms and protect our rights.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use client customer data in a Business Suite for our own marketing. Our team has technical access to data stored in client Business Suites, websites and automations, but we use it only as described in Section 7.5.

3Legal Bases

6What We Never Ask For

Our onboarding forms will never ask you to enter passwords, full card numbers, bank account numbers or Social Security numbers. When we need access to a domain, website or account, we ask you to add us as a user or delegate, or to share access through a secure method we explain separately. If anyone claiming to be ProyTech asks for these items by email, text or form, do not send them, and contact us.

7Client Data in a Business Suite or Client Website

7.1 We act on our client's behalf. When we host a Business Suite, website or automation for a client, the client decides what information about its own customers and leads is collected and how it is used. For that data, we are a service provider (sometimes called a processor) acting on the client's instructions, and the client's privacy policy applies.

7.2 What we do with it. We process client customer data only to provide, secure, support and maintain the Services for that client, as required by law, or as the client instructs. We do not sell it, use it to market to those customers, or combine it with other clients' data.

7.3 Requests from a client's customers. If you are a customer of one of our clients and want to access, correct or delete your information, please contact that business directly. If you contact us, we will forward your request to the client.

7.4 Client responsibilities. Clients are responsible for having a privacy policy on their website, obtaining required consents, and handling their customers' requests.

7.5 We can access your data, but we don't use it. Because we build, host and support your Business Suite, website and automations, our team has technical access to the information stored in them, including your customers' and leads' information. We do not access, review, use, sell, rent or share that information for any purpose of our own, including our own marketing or to train AI models. We access it only when needed to provide, secure, support, back up or maintain the Services; when you ask us to (for example, a support request); or when the law requires it. Access is limited to ProyTech team members who need it for those purposes and is protected by the safeguards in Section 10.

8Mortgage Lender Clients and GLBA

8.1 Some clients are mortgage lenders or loan originators subject to the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) and the FTC Safeguards Rule. For those clients, we are a service provider. We use the nonpublic personal information we receive only to perform the Services, do not disclose or reuse it except as the GLBA allows, and maintain safeguards appropriate to it.

8.2 Our Business Suite is designed for lead and relationship management, not for collecting loan applications. Clients should not use it to store Social Security numbers, full account numbers, credit reports or loan application files, and our configuration avoids fields for them. 10Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including: encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest through our hosting and database providers; database row-level security so each client and user sees only their own data; role-based access limited to people who need it; multi-factor authentication on our administrative accounts; rate limiting and monitoring on our applications; and vendor selection based on security practices. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

11Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and CRM use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for the site to work (for example, keeping you signed in) and to understand how the site is used (analytics). If we use advertising pixels in the future, we will update this policy and provide any required choices. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work without them. Our site does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals.

13Your Choices and Rights

13.1 Choices for everyone. You may: ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you; ask us to correct it; ask us to delete it, subject to legal and contractual retention needs; unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in each email; and stop texts by replying STOP.

13.2 State privacy rights. Depending on where you live, state law may give you additional rights, such as the right to know, access, correct, delete or port your data and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising or profiling. We will honor requests as the applicable law requires, verify your identity before acting, and not discriminate against you for using your rights. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision.

13.3 How to make a request. Email us at admin@getproytech.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We aim to respond within 30 days, or within the time the applicable law requires.

14Kansas Residents

ProyTech is based in Kansas. If a security breach involves personal information of Kansas residents as defined by Kansas law, we will provide notice as required by the Kansas data breach notification statute (K.S.A. 50-7a01 et seq.).
