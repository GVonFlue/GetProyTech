        How the grade works: 12 areas worth 100 points. Each check passes, partly passes or misses. Anything we could not check is left out and the rest is scaled to 100. A is 90 or more, B 80 to 89, C 70 to 79, D 60 to 69, F below 60.

        What it grades

        The first five seconds

        Whether a new visitor on a phone can tell what you do, where, and what to do next, and whether the top of the page shows your real work.

        Trust and buyer psychology

        Proof, reviews and credentials, and the levers that make people act: naming their problem, selling the result, why you, taking the risk off, and an honest reason to call now.

        The customer journey

        How a visitor moves through your pages: a way to reach you on every page, a next step at the end of each, and a contact form that doesn't ask for too much.

        Pages, Google and AI assistants

        A page for each service and area, titles and descriptions on every page, your sitemap, and whether AI assistants are allowed to read your site at all.

        Behind the scenes

        What your site runs on and what catches the lead: the CRM, booking, chat, analytics, ad pixels and call tracking we can see, plus your email domain's protection.
